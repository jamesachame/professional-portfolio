The questions people ask me first

Straight answers, so the call can start somewhere useful.

Do I have to rebuild my whole setup?

No. Most of the time the fastest wins come from fixing the gaps between what you already have: a form that doesn’t trigger anything, a pipeline stage nobody moves deals out of, tracking that was never finished. We start there. A full rebuild only happens if the audit says it’s genuinely cheaper than patching.

I already use GoHighLevel. Can you work inside it?

That’s where I do most of my work. Pipelines, workflows, SMS and email sequences, calendars, forms and custom API apps built on GHL. I’ll work in your existing sub-account and leave it documented so your team can maintain it.

How long does it take?

A landing page with a qualifying funnel is usually live in days. A full lead system (pages, pipelines, follow-up, integrations and tracking) typically runs a couple of weeks depending on how many tools have to be connected. You get a scope and a timeline in writing after the consultation, before anything starts.

What does it cost?

It depends entirely on scope, which is exactly why the consultation is free and comes first. You’ll get a fixed price for a defined build, with no open-ended hourly billing and no surprise invoices.

Do I own everything when we’re done?

Yes. Everything is built in your accounts, on your domains, under your logins. You keep the pages, the workflows, the integrations and the documentation. Nothing is held hostage on my side.

I’m not sure what should be automated yet. Is it too early to talk?

That’s the most common reason people book. The consultation is a working session, not a sales call. We map how leads move through your business and find where the time and money are actually going. You leave with a prioritised list whether or not you hire me.
